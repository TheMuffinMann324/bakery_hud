3D minimap renderer by Geckomacabre, from GTA-VI-Inspired-HUD-for-FiveM.
Map geometry and ground survey by loneeps (@moddims), used in the FiveM port with their permission.
The accompanying blip atlas uses GTA V blip artwork sourced from the Cfx.re documentation.

Upstream revision b5de49845c5ffec6e209f18691c535f59f02676f

https://www.gta5-mods.com/scripts/gtavi-minimap-3d-buildings-legacy-enhanced

Bakery HUD packages map3d.js as renderer.js and adapts its blip draw pass to keep
markers above buildings, retain sprite and color data, and draw full sprite batches.
The canvas uses subpixel layout dimensions to align the painted map to the HUD.
Bakery HUD can draw the map badge in the shared overlay for both radar modes.
The map data and artwork files are unchanged.
